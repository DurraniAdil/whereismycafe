# Where Is My Café? (WIMC) — Detailed Business Model & Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary: The Experience Gap
Google Maps solves for "Where," but fails to solve for "How it feels." WIMC bridges this gap by mapping the *ambience*. 

**WIMC Studio** is the revenue arm that transforms cafés from "hidden gems" into "city icons" by owning their visual narrative and placement within the WIMC ecosystem.

---

## 2. Revenue Architecture: The "Growth" Stack

### Stream 1: App Native Advertising (The Internal Moat)
We sell visibility within the high-intent environment of the WIMC App.
*   **Vibe Takeover (₹1,999/mo):** Exclusive "Sponsored" slot at the top of a specific category (e.g., being the first café shown for "Date Spot").
*   **Dynamic Push Pings (₹500/campaign):** High-converting notifications sent to local users based on weather or time (e.g., "Cold brew weather! Visit [Café] for a discount").
*   **The "Verifed ✅" Badge (₹299/mo):** A psychological trust trigger that increases click-through rates by 2.5x.

### Stream 2: WIMC Studio (Visual & Social Authority)
Café owners don't want "SEO"; they want "Vibe." 
*   **Content "Drop" Packages (₹3,500 - ₹7,500):** Professional 9:16 Reel/Shorts shoots specifically designed to trend.
*   **Aesthetic Audit:** Consulting on lighting, music, and "Instagrammability."
*   **Digital Menu & Story:** Building a high-conversion "Link-in-bio" landing page that works better than a traditional website.

### Stream 3: Physical Brand Assets (The "Real World" Bridge)
Branded touchpoints inside the café that link back to the app.
*   **WIMC QR Kits (₹999/kit):** Premium, heavy-duty table standees with QR codes that drive users to their WIMC profile for reviews and "Vibe Tagging."
*   **"Top Rated on WIMC" Stickers:** Building real-world social proof similar to Tripadvisor.

### Stream 4: Data as a Service (B2B Insights)
Selling local market intelligence that Google doesn't share.
*   **Trend Reports (₹2,500/qtr):** "What is the city searching for?" (e.g., "Matcha search volume doubled in CIDCO this month—time to update your menu").
*   **Benchmark Dashboard:** Real-time data on how they compare to the "Top 5" cafés in their area.

## 3. The Trust-Based Onboarding Strategy (Avoiding the "Trap")
To avoid being seen as "just another agency," we lead with value and evidence before asking for significant investment.

### Phase 1: The "Gift-First" Onboarding (Day 1-30)
*   **Goal:** Maximum Goodwill.
*   **Action:** Onboard them to the **Free Tier** manually. Don't send a link; go there, show them the app, and say: *"We've already listed you because we love your vibe. Here is how you can claim it for free."*
*   **Key Feature:** The "Live View" — They can see people discovering them for ₹0.

### Phase 2: The "Micro-Win" Trial (Day 30-60)
*   **Goal:** Prove the "Studio" quality without a high-ticket barrier.
*   **The Offer:** The **"90-Minute Refresh" (₹999)**. A one-time session to take 5 high-end photos and 1 trending Reel.
*   **Target:** Owners who are active on Instagram but struggling with quality.

### Phase 3: The "Strategic Partner" (Day 60+)
*   **Goal:** Long-term recurring revenue.
*   **The Pitch:** Transition from "The guy who takes photos" to "The brain behind your growth." Use the app data (Stream 4) to justify the investment in higher tiers.

We don't sell "Local SEO." We sell **"The Visibility System."**

*   **Stage 3: Convert** — Clear paths to footfall (Maps integration, WhatsApp booking, and exclusive perks).
